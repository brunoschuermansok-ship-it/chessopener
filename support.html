Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open Profile → Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought Premium with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your device's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → ChessOpener and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my progress saved if I change phones?

Your progress is stored on your device and is included in your iCloud or computer backup. Restoring that backup on your new device brings your progress with it.

How do I erase my data?

Open Profile → Settings → Erase all my data. This removes your progress, courses and settings from the device.

The opening explorer doesn't load.

The explorer uses lichess.org and needs an internet connection. If Lichess asks for sign-in, create a free token at lichess.org/account/oauth/token (no permissions needed) and paste it in Settings → Opening explorer.

I found a mistake in a course.

Please email us the course name and the moves; we'll fix it in the next update.
