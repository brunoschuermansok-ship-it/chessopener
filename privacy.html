Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

ChessOpener is made by Bruno Schuermans ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the ChessOpener app for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

We do not collect any personal data. ChessOpener has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your training progress, review schedule, streaks, settings and any courses you create are saved locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer backup) according to your own device settings. We cannot see this data. You can erase it at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Erase all my data, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so Premium features can be unlocked. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Optional online features

Some features contact public chess services directly from your device, only when you use them:

  • Lichess opening explorer and cloud evaluation (analysis board): the chess position you are looking at is sent to lichess.org to fetch move statistics and an evaluation.
  • Importing a Lichess study: the study link you enter is sent to lichess.org to download it.
  • Fetching your rating (optional, during setup or in your profile): the Chess.com or Lichess username you type is sent to that service's public API to read your public rating.

These requests go straight from your device to those services; we do not receive or store them. If you choose to paste a Lichess API token in Settings, it is stored only on your device and sent only to lichess.org. These services have their own privacy policies: Lichess, Chess.com.

Coach and AI

Move explanations are generated on your device, using Apple's on-device language model where available. Nothing you ask the coach leaves your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers and do not send marketing messages.

Children

ChessOpener is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you live in the EU or UK you still have rights under the GDPR; you can contact us with any question and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If a future version adds features that collect data (for example optional accounts or leaderboards), we will update this policy before releasing that version and show the change date at the top.
