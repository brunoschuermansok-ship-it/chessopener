Community Guidelines

Effective 21 October 2026

These guidelines apply to any course you create in ChessOpener and, when sharing is available, to courses you share with other players.

  • Courses must be about chess. Keep titles, descriptions and comments relevant and respectful.
  • No hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or otherwise objectionable content, and no personal information about other people.
  • Only use material you have the right to use. Don't copy commercial courses or books.
  • No spam, advertising or links to unrelated sites.

Content that breaks these rules can be reported in the app and will be removed; accounts that repeatedly break them can be blocked. Questions: brunoschuermansok@gmail.com.
